Intelligent Stewart Platform for Payload Stabilization

A six-degree-of-freedom parallel-platform concept exploring coordinated actuation, IMU-based orientation sensing, inverse kinematics, and feedback control.

Development stage: Early-stage design and simulation.

What we're exploring

01 · Mechanical design

A fixed base, moving platform, six actuators, and articulated joints designed in CAD.

02 · Kinematics

Mapping a desired platform pose to actuator lengths while respecting mechanical limits.

03 · Simulation

Using MATLAB to study platform motion, disturbances, and tracking performance.

04 · Feedback control

Investigating how orientation measurements can inform corrective actuator commands.

Potential applications

Camera mounts, sensor platforms, and other payloads that may benefit from orientation stabilization.

Planned evaluation

Orientation error, settling time, overshoot, repeatability, and actuator/joint-limit compliance.

This is an early-stage student project. The design and control approach are under development; no performance claims are made before validation.
